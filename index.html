The ANCA Approach

A Focused Environment for Meaningful Learning

Abhishek Naag Coaching Academy is a Commerce-focused coaching academy based in Kopar Khairane, Navi Mumbai. The academy serves students across school and foundation-level academic pathways.

Our learning structure is designed to support concept clarity, regular practice, personal attention, and exam-focused preparation.
